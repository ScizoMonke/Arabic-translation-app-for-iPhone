# PLAN-5: live top half (D43)

1. Mechanism: own rolling window, not WhisperKit's AudioStreamTranscriber (at 1bbc57cb it opens its own microphone through AudioProcessor.startRecordingLive, keeps every sample since start, and confirms only when a decode has more than two segments; it cannot honour the hold seal). MicCapture's energy gate stays as the speech detector, but its samples now go into a lock-guarded LiveWindow (audio since the last confirmation point) instead of finished utterances.
2. Loop (AppModel, own task): about every 1 s of new speech (2 s on large-v3), snapshot the window, Whisper transcribe ar with timestamps, confirm a prefix of segments, advance the window past them, Apple-translate the confirmed Arabic (appended, immutable) and the unconfirmed tail (shown lighter). One decode at a time; a tick that arrives during a decode is skipped. The mic only appends under a lock and never waits.
3. Confirmation: never the last segment of a decode; a segment settles when its text matches the same-position segment of the previous decode of the same window, or when it ended 4 s or more before the window end, or the window has reached 24 s. When the energy gate closes an utterance (0.8 s quiet) a final decode of exactly that audio settles everything. The window advances to the start of the first unsettled segment; a generation counter, bumped by every hold edge and every blip removal, throws away results from a window that changed under the decode.
4. Models: turbo 632MB (openai_whisper-large-v3-v20240930_turbo_632MB) for live, large-v3 947MB for today's path; both downloaded at setup, only one loaded. Pack installed -> turbo live; turbo refuses to load -> large-v3 live at 2 s cadence; pack missing (after setup) -> large-v3 translate per utterance with the English prompt and NL check (the only place they survive).
5. Pack gate: after downloads and before the split screen, setup stops until Apple's Arabic pack is installed (first setup only; a flag remembers it). "Unsupported on this phone" offers the slower path instead of a dead end.
6. Translate calls serialized inside AppleTranslator (FIFO chain), R24.
7. Log: THEM line per settled segment with Arabic escaped and English; TIME them per settled segment (speech-start, first-live, settle, ms); TIME line also on fallback-path failures (R29).
8. Files: MicCapture.swift, Transcriber.swift, AppleTranslator.swift, AppModel.swift, ContentView.swift, FIRST_RUN.md. No new Swift file unless the window logic will not fit.
9. Most likely to break on first compile: Sendable/actor crossings around the lock-guarded window and the segment arrays passed out of the Transcriber actor; DecodingOptions argument order; @unknown default in the new pack-gate switch.
10. Most likely to break on first run: turbo load refused or slow (R31), boundary repeats when Whisper's segment timestamps drift, and Apple Translation choking on short Egyptian fragments (R30). A Python twin of the confirmation rule runs on the PC with faster-whisper turbo on bench clips before the Swift is final.
